## Summary

Guidelines for citing DataJoint in publications. The primary DataJoint paper is Yatsenko et al. (2015) published as a preprint and formalized in subsequent publications. DataJoint Elements have separate citations. MATLAB DataJoint has its own citation.

## Key Claims

- The primary citation for DataJoint (Python) is the Yatsenko et al. foundational paper
- DataJoint Elements pipelines (calcium imaging, electrophysiology) have module-specific citations
- Researchers should cite both the DataJoint framework and any Elements they use
- BibTeX format is provided for all major components
