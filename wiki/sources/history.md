## Summary

DataJoint originated in 2009 at Baylor College of Medicine under Dimitri Yatsenko for neuroscience data management. The Python version launched in 2015. Vathes LLC was formed in 2016 to commercialize it. In 2022 NIH funded DataJoint Elements. In 2024 DataJoint Inc. was incorporated. In 2025 a $4.9M seed round was raised.

## Key Claims

- 2009: First DataJoint implementation (MATLAB) by Dimitri Yatsenko at Baylor College of Medicine
- 2015: Python version released; open-sourced on GitHub
- 2016: Vathes LLC formed to support commercial development
- 2022: NIH-funded DataJoint Elements project begins — standardized neuroscience pipelines
- 2024: DataJoint Inc. incorporated as successor to Vathes LLC
- 2025: $4.9M seed funding raised to expand the platform

## Key Quotes

> "DataJoint began as a solution to a specific problem: how do you manage complex neuroscience experiments across multiple researchers without losing data provenance?"
